What's a build plate, and why does it matter?

The build plate is the metal sheet your print sticks to. Different plates are designed for different materials. PLA sticks best to textured PEI or Cool Plate. PETG and ABS need engineering or high-temp plates. Using the wrong plate is the most common cause of prints peeling off mid-job, or worse, not coming off at all.

To swap plates, lift the current sheet off the magnet, place the new one on top, and align it to the back-left corner.
